LongBox Support

LongBox is an offline comic reader for your CBZ, CBR and PDF files.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, email JacobRam1993@gmail.com.

When reporting a problem, include your device, iOS version, app version and the steps that caused it. Please do not send passwords, private network addresses or copyrighted comic files. TestFlight testers can also send feedback through TestFlight.

Getting started

Open Settings > Import Comics to import from Files/iCloud or your local SMB network. Guided lessons are available in Learn LongBox. Imported comics are available offline.
