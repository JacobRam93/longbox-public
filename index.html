LongBox Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Contact: JacobRam1993@gmail.com

LongBox is a comic reader that imports files for offline reading. It does not require a LongBox account, contain advertising or an analytics SDK, track you across apps, or send your library to a developer-operated server.

Your library

Comic archives, covers, reading progress, preferences, import history and statistics are stored on your device. Files you choose through Files may be downloaded by their provider, including iCloud. Device backups and file-provider services operate according to your Apple and provider settings.

Local network shares

When you choose a NAS, LongBox connects to that server to authenticate, browse folders and download comics. The server receives the requests needed to perform those actions. Saved credentials are stored in the device-only, non-synchronizing iOS Keychain, accessible while unlocked. Removing a saved share removes its saved login credentials; offline comics remain. LongBox does not send these credentials to the developer. SMB encryption is enabled by default; disabling it changes protection for network transfers.

Camera

Optional Front Screen Book Mode requests camera permission and runs a camera session to support the outer display. LongBox does not record or save camera footage, use the microphone, or upload camera frames. The app’s camera toggle stops this feature; revoke system permission in iOS Settings if desired.

Notifications

Optional local notifications report import completion, interruption or failure. LongBox has no remote push-notification server. You can change notification permission in iOS Settings.

Device information

LongBox reads available storage to display capacity and check import space, file metadata to manage selected/local files, app preferences, and elapsed time to enforce network timeouts. These are not used for advertising or fingerprinting.

Deletion and feedback

Delete comics within LongBox or use File Management to manage stored archives. Removing the app deletes its app container; iOS-managed Keychain credentials may persist, so remove saved shares first to clear logins. Import history and lifetime reading statistics can be separate from individual comic files. TestFlight automatically provides crash logs and usage data to Apple and the app developer under Apple’s TestFlight terms; this collection cannot be opted out of while using the TestFlight beta. These diagnostics may include device identifiers, software/hardware details and network information. They are used to investigate problems and improve LongBox. Apple handles its copy under Apple’s privacy policy. Information you choose to send in beta feedback or email is used to investigate and respond; avoid sending passwords or private comic files.

Contact the email above for privacy questions or to request deletion of feedback you have sent. This policy will be updated if the app’s data practices change.
